Add your songs to the shared library

  1. Go to exportify.net and log in with Spotify.
  2. Click Export next to Liked Songs or any playlist. You get a .csv file.
  3. Upload that file here. Everyone can then build playlists with the songs.

Privacy: only song information is kept (title, artists, album, tempo, key, genres). Your Spotify username and the dates you saved songs are never stored, and the library doesn't record who uploaded what.

Library status

After an import, songs are usable right away with the data in your CSV. BPM matches, extra tags and genres are added in the background, and big imports can take a while to finish.

Enrichment queue

Nothing waiting. Every song has been looked up.
